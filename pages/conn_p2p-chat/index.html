How are you joining?

Two people open this same page. One creates a room, the other joins.
No server is involved — the connection is directly between your browsers.

Step 1 of 2 — Share this offer

Copy the text below and send it to the person you want to chat with (paste, email, DM — anything works).

Step 2 of 2 — Paste their answer

After they paste your offer on their page, they'll get an answer to send back to you. Paste it here.

Step 1 of 2 — Paste the offer
